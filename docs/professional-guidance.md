# When To Get Professional Guidance

Status: Personal-project safety guidance  
Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

GoodToGo is an organizer. It helps identify what exists, what is missing, where things
are located, and who may need to act. It does not replace professional legal, tax,
financial, or medical advice.

## Legal Guidance

Consider an estate-planning attorney, elder-law attorney, probate attorney, or other
qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction when you need to:

- Create, update, revoke, interpret, or validate a will, trust, codicil, power of
  attorney, health care directive, guardian nomination, deed, beneficiary designation, or
  other legal document.
- Decide who should serve as executor, trustee, guardian, health care agent, financial
  agent, or digital fiduciary.
- Plan for minor children, adult dependents, blended families, estranged family members,
  disability benefits, business ownership, real estate, large debts, litigation, or assets
  in more than one state or country.
- Understand what authority another person actually has during incapacity or after death.
- Handle probate, estate administration, trust administration, creditor notices, or asset
  distribution after someone dies.

## Medical Guidance

Consider a clinician, advance-care planning specialist, palliative-care professional,
hospice professional, social worker, or patient advocate when you need to:

- Understand treatment options, care preferences, prognosis, or medical tradeoffs.
- Complete or review advance directives, living wills, health care powers of attorney, or
  medical orders such as DNR, POLST, MOLST, or state equivalents.
- Discuss capacity, dementia planning, serious illness, long-term care, hospice, organ or
  tissue donation, or body donation.
- Make sure your chosen health care agent understands your values and practical care
  preferences.

## Tax And Financial Guidance

Consider a CPA, tax professional, fiduciary financial planner, benefits specialist, or
other qualified advisor when you need to:

- Understand estate, inheritance, gift, income, retirement-account, or property-tax
  consequences.
- Review account ownership, beneficiary designations, insurance coverage, long-term-care
  funding, business succession, or special-needs planning.
- Decide whether an action affects public benefits, creditor exposure, or family support
  obligations.

## What GoodToGo Should Store

Store status, locations, responsible people, review dates, source links, and safe notes.
Do not store raw passwords, MFA seeds, recovery codes, safe combinations, or private
cryptographic keys.
