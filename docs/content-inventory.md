# Affairs-in-Order Content Inventory

Status: Broad product inventory for content design and professional review

This document defines the subjects the application should eventually cover. It is not a
claim that every user needs every document or action.

Each item should support:

- Applicable, not applicable, or uncertain
- Readiness state
- Individual, joint, household, or external ownership
- Responsible person
- Authoritative original location
- Optional encrypted copy
- Last verified and next review dates
- Notes safe to include in selected exports
- Follow-up task and official resource links

## Survivor playbook

The product should eventually provide a separate action sequence:

### First hours and days

- Contact appropriate medical or emergency authority
- Notify close family and responsible roles
- Arrange dependent and pet care
- Locate authoritative disposition instructions
- Secure home, vehicles, wallet, devices, and valuables
- Preserve phone and email access without impersonating the deceased
- Contact funeral provider where appropriate
- Request certified death certificates

### First weeks

- Contact attorney and executor
- Notify employer and benefit administrators
- Notify insurers and begin eligible claims
- Contact financial and tax professionals
- Maintain essential mortgage, insurance, utilities, and property security
- Contact Social Security, VA, or other agencies as applicable
- Inventory assets, liabilities, and ownership
- Preserve documents and records

### Following months

- Follow probate or trust administration
- File insurance and survivor-benefit claims
- File required tax returns
- Transfer titles and ownership
- Review dependent health coverage
- Close or transfer digital and financial accounts with authority
- Cancel nonessential subscriptions
- Notify credit bureaus and monitor identity misuse
- Update the surviving household's own plan

This workflow requires professional legal and tax review before release.
