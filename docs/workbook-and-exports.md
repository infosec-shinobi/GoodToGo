# Workbook, Take-Home List, and Export Design

Status: First printable workbook outline and export requirements

## Experience principles

- Calm, direct language
- Small sections rather than one enormous form
- “Not sure” is a productive answer
- Explain the next action, not only the gap
- Never shame users with a death-readiness score
- Make completion possible over multiple sessions
- Separate legal authority, information location, and personal preference
- Make privacy consequences clear before saving or exporting

## Digital workbook structure

### Cover and orientation

- Purpose
- Educational-use disclaimer
- Privacy mode explanation
- Do not enter raw secrets warning
- Estimated time by section
- How Ready, In progress, Not started, Not sure, and Not applicable work

### Section 1: People and responsibilities

- Executor and backup
- Health care agent
- Financial agent
- Trustees
- Guardians and caregivers
- Digital fiduciary
- Professional contacts
- Agreement and confirmation status

### Section 2: Legal and health readiness

- Will and trust status
- Financial power of attorney
- Advance directive and health care power of attorney
- Medical-information permissions
- Guardianship
- Donation and disposition documents
- Signed-original locations

### Section 3: Financial and insurance inventory

- Assets and income
- Debts and recurring transactions
- Beneficiaries and account ownership
- Insurance policies and claims contacts
- Tax and employer benefits
- Business interests

### Section 4: Digital life

- Identity recovery chain
- Password-manager emergency access
- Google and Apple legacy settings
- Social and professional presence
- Photos, cloud storage, and backups
- Domains, websites, and repositories
- Financial-value accounts and cryptocurrency
- Devices and data disposition

### Section 5: Household continuity

- Utilities and essential recurring services
- Dependents and pets
- Shutoffs and emergency contacts
- Security system and smart home
- Router, Wi-Fi, computers, NAS, backups, and self-hosted services
- Trusted vendors and recurring household responsibilities

### Section 6: Wishes and handoff

- Funeral and memorial preferences
- Obituary and notification information
- Cultural and spiritual wishes
- Sentimental property and legacy material
- Survival-package location
- Trusted-person access test
- Review schedule

## Personalized take-home list

The list is generated from Not started and Not sure answers. In persistent mode, users
may also include In progress items.

Each task contains:

- Checkbox
- Priority
- Plain-language title
- One concrete next step
- Section
- Responsible person, when known
- Target date, when selected
- Official setup or reference links
- Space for notes in print

Tasks should sort by:

1. Immediate authority and dependent-care gaps
2. Identity and access recovery gaps
3. Financial and insurance discovery gaps
4. Household continuity
5. Final wishes and long-term refinements

The private-session implementation generates this list locally in the browser. It does
not send answers to the server.

## Printable blank workbook

The blank workbook is a standalone handout for nontechnical users. It should contain:

- Plain-language questions
- Ready, needs work, not sure, and not applicable checkboxes
- Lines for safe notes
- “Where is the original?” rather than “Upload this”
- Credential-locator fields rather than password fields
- Tear-out take-home task pages
- A resource appendix
- Version, jurisdiction, and review date
- Page number and confidentiality footer

Do not include QR codes containing personal data or secrets. A QR code may point to a
public official guide.

## Completed export profiles

### Emergency and incapacity

- Emergency contacts
- Health care agents and document locations
- Essential health information
- Dependent and pet care
- Home access and immediate safety instructions

### Household continuity

- Essential utilities and billing continuity
- Household vendors
- Property operations
- Home technology overview
- Technical-helper contact

### Executor and survivor

- Responsible roles
- Legal-document locations
- Asset, liability, insurance, and benefit inventories
- Notification list
- Sequenced survivor checklist

### Digital fiduciary

- Provider and identifying account
- Credential locator
- MFA and recovery topology
- Legacy feature status
- Preserve, export, memorialize, transfer, close, or delete instructions
- Official survivor guides

### Full private archive

- Authorized complete data
- Uploaded document copies
- Machine-readable records
- Manifest and integrity hashes
- Plain-language recovery instructions
- Content and schema versions

## Print requirements

- US Letter first; add A4 later
- Black-and-white usable
- No color-only meaning
- Page breaks before major sections
- Avoid splitting a task or question across pages
- Display full URLs in the resource appendix
- Generation date and source pack on every completed export
- Sensitivity marking and intended audience
- Optional footer identifying the owner or household
- Redacted identifiers by default

## Export safety confirmation

Before a completed sensitive export, show:

- Intended audience
- Included subjects
- Included sections
- Private records excluded or included
- Identifier-redaction setting
- Document attachments included or excluded
- Storage and printer warning
