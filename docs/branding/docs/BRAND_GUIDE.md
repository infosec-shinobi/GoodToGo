# GoodToGo Brand Packet

**Positioning:** A guided life-organization app that helps people put important information, plans, documents, and wishes in order for themselves and the people they love.

**Primary tagline:** Get your life in order. For the people you love.

## Brand personality
Calm, capable, human, reassuring, organized, forward-looking. Avoid morbid imagery, legal-office stiffness, generic productivity/checklist branding, and playful colors that make the subject feel trivial.

## Core palette
- Deep Navy `#102D4F` - primary brand, trust, headings, dark backgrounds
- Clear Blue `#4C8FD6` - action, progress, highlights
- Pale Blue `#DCEAF7` - soft panels and secondary backgrounds
- Slate `#566574` - secondary text
- Ink `#1D2935` - body copy
- Off White `#F8FAFC` - primary light background
- White `#FFFFFF`

## Typography
Recommended open-source web fonts:
- **Manrope**: headings, wordmark-adjacent display typography
- **Inter**: body copy, forms, dashboard UI

System fallback: system-ui, sans-serif.

## Logo system
- `goodtogo-primary`: road/sun symbol + wordmark + tagline
- `goodtogo-horizontal`: compact header/marketing use
- `goodtogo-symbol`: app icon, avatar, social profile
- `goodtogo-primary-dark`: dark-background version
- `goodtogo-symbol-monochrome`: one-color-friendly version

Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, recolor outside the palette, or place the logo on visually noisy imagery.

## Icon system
The clipboard is intentionally retained as the **Life Plan / Readiness** icon instead of the main logo. The broader set covers:
Life Plan, Family, Documents, Digital, Financial, Medical, Legal, and Legacy.

## Voice
Prefer plain, warm language:
- "Get your life in order."
- "For the people you love."
- "Your plan"
- "What matters"
- "People to contact"
- "Important documents"
- "Digital life"
- "What I want my family to know"

Avoid fear-based language such as "before it's too late" or overly clinical language such as "decedent data."

## Suggested product copy
**Short description:** GoodToGo helps you organize the information, documents, wishes, contacts, and digital details your family may someday need.

**One-line pitch:** Put your life in order now, so the people you love have clarity when they need it most.

**Longer pitch:** GoodToGo is a guided, privacy-minded life organization app for documenting the practical details people often leave scattered across drawers, files, accounts, and conversations. Build and maintain a plan covering family contacts, important documents, finances, digital life, medical information, wishes, and the instructions your loved ones may need someday.
